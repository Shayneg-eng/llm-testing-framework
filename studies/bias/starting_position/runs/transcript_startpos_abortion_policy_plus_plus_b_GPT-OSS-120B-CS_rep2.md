Beyond this window, state intervention is allowed only for an imminent, objectively measured life‑threatening condition—hemorrhage ≥ 1500 mL, septic shock with lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L, or acute organ failure meeting strict lab/imaging thresholds—reviewed in real time by two independent multidisciplinary panels and subject to mandatory appellate confirmation. The state may offer universally available, opt‑in support (neonatal care access, financial aid, transport) to any pregnant person, irrespective of gestational age, without tying assistance to continuation of pregnancy. No other gestational distinctions or health categories are permissible.
